About Winston

Finance leadership grounded in operating reality.

I am a senior finance executive and FP&A leader focused on helping organizations connect strategy, operations, and financial accountability.

Across complex businesses and periods of change, I have built planning processes, operating models, executive reporting, and decision frameworks designed to make the business clearer, not simply produce more numbers.

I believe strong finance organizations combine analytical discipline with judgment, service, and the courage to challenge assumptions constructively. My writing explores how leaders allocate resources, respond to uncertainty, develop people, and create the conditions for better decisions.
