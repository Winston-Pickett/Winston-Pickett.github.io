Inside this edition

Diplomatic escalation, proposed sanctions and verified operational follow-ups.

The November 23 edition distinguishes Russia's confirmed diplomatic protest against Estonia from contested allegations about Ukrainian drone operations, and the European Commission's proposed 22nd sanctions package from enacted restrictions. It records Ukraine's November 23 acknowledgment of its November 22 drone strike on Russian naval assets in Benghazi, where Libyan officials report severe damage but Ukraine's claim that a corvette sank remains independently unresolved. Finnish Border Guard reporting confirms that towing of the damaged Sparta II began November 22 with no detected hazardous spill; this is an update to Saturday's event, not a new strike. Reuters reports an official Dymer death toll of 11 for the November 22 apartment strike, alongside the rescue of a child and elderly woman. Russian officials also announced a prospective 47 billion ruble rail-ferry programme for Kaliningrad. The 97-page complete edition preserves ten sections, 373 selected dated/overlap records, 22 separate date-limited disclosures, nine stacked readiness indicators, and 657 PDF source links, with a November 23, 11:26 a.m. EST research cutoff.
