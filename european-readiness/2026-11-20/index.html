Inside this edition

Verified operational disruptions and decisions, with competing interpretations separated.

The November 20 intraday edition documents a large reported Russian missile and drone attack on Ukrainian bridges, electricity, rail and water infrastructure, with official casualty and launch counts separately attributed. Polish authorities reported three Russian-origin objects entering national airspace and falling on Polish territory, without reported domestic casualties or critical-infrastructure damage. The European Union Council adopted two €1 billion European Peace Facility procurement decisions, while the additional €45 billion proposed Ukraine financing package remains subject to approval. A November 9-20 multinational Coalition of the Willing exercise is identified as defensive contingency preparation, not an actual post-ceasefire deployment. Diplomatic statements following Berlin are materially opposed: a U.S. envoy suggested Russian comfort with Article 5-like guarantees while a Russian deputy minister rejected quasi-membership. The complete 92-page report has ten sections, 362 selected rolling-register records, 22 separately retained date-limited disclosures, nine standing indicators and 647 clickable PDF links.
