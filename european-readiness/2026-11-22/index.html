Inside this edition

Evidence-graded security reporting and business continuity analysis.

Sunday's edition documents the November 22 strike on a residential building in Dymer north of Kyiv, where rescue and casualty totals remained provisional. It follows the November 21 Sparta II vessel incident and Finnish maritime response as a continuing situation, not a second maritime strike. Finnish authorities reported a suspected helicopter airspace violation near Loviisa, while the Kaliningrad governor reported a separate air alert disputed by Lithuania. Poland's November 20 airspace event remains a dated baseline; NATO's November 21 reassurance and planned November 23 consultations are diplomatic responses rather than Article 5 activation.
