Inside this edition

Evidence-graded security reporting and business continuity analysis.

Saturday's edition documents the November 21 Sparta II drone strike and fire in the Gulf of Finland, including Finland's precautionary air and maritime restrictions without claiming a Baltic-wide closure. It distinguishes the European Commission's actual €7.9 billion Ukraine Facility disbursement on November 20 from proposed future aid. Denmark's expulsion of a Russian defence attaché and proposed Schengen visa changes are separately attributed, with intelligence allegations unresolved. The Poland airspace incident remains carried forward from Friday, and the Russian border-force assessment does not establish a new combat-ready T-90M deployment.
