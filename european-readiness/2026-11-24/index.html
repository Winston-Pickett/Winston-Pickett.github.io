Inside this edition

Protective security, dated military construction and verified operational disruption.

The November 24 edition reports a new joint UK NPSA/MI5 and Ministry of Defence security alert to companies supplying Ukraine's armed forces, with public government confirmation of executive briefings on November 24-25. It adds original Yle satellite-image reporting published November 23 on extensive 2025-2026 reconstruction at Russia's Kamenka garrison near Finland, carefully distinguishing a proposed 11,000-person divisional capacity from personnel actually stationed and equipped. Poland's November 23 official NATO account confirms an extraordinary Council meeting and defensive AWACS deployment following November 20 missile incursions. November 24 Ukrainian reports identify a deadly Zaporizhzhia residential strike and attack-related electricity interruptions in five regions, separate from outages caused by weather. The European Commission's 22nd sanctions package remains a proposal, not adopted law; Libya's response to the November 22 Benghazi maritime incident is a diplomatic update, not a new attack. The complete 99-page source-linked report maintains all ten sections, 377 dated or verified-overlap register records, 22 separate date-limited disclosures, nine stacked readiness indicators and 663 clickable source links.

Evidence limits: Britain's sabotage warning is an official assessment, not proof that all suppliers have been attacked.
