Inside this edition

Business continuity decisions grounded in verified events.

The November 18 edition updates the Bulgaria LPG carrier GAS HUSKY investigation: Reuters reported that a regional governor described four strikes before the November 17 sinking and all 19 crew rescued, while the responsible actor and weapon type remain unconfirmed. It analyzes NATO's November 17 Ukraine Defence Contact Group announcements on air defence, ammunition, drones and PURL support, distinguishing pledges from disbursements and deliveries. Allied disagreement over deep-strike policy is discussed as a policy difference, not an operational war order. A November 18 account of Ukraine's Operation Vivaldi concerns a mostly October operation and is not misdated as a new military action. The country-by-country business outlook ties maritime insurance, actual logistics routes, power continuity, sanctions and procurement to specific decision triggers. The complete 90-page edition retains ten sections, a 355-record selected rolling register, 22 separate date-limited disclosures and nine standing indicators.
