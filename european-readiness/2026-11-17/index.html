Inside this edition

Verified disruptions, not extrapolated conflict.

The November 17 edition analyzes the reported second Kerch Bridge strike and traffic restrictions, Kyiv's transport and electricity disruptions, Hungary's two-day suspension of rail service with Ukraine, and Bulgaria's reported tanker sinking and crew rescue operation. It also distinguishes the EU Council's November 16 sanctions actions from actual transaction impacts and Lithuanian NATO rotation from Russian offensive mobilization. Damage and attribution claims are labelled according to their evidentiary status. The two-month selected register contains 355 dated or verified-overlap records, with 22 separate date-limited disclosures, and nine standing indicators. This is a business-continuity assessment, not an official warning or prediction of wider war.
