Inside this edition

Energy infrastructure under pressure, newly adopted EU trade controls and proportional business readiness.

The November 25 European Readiness Daily examines newly reported Russian strikes on Ukrainian energy facilities and a gas-production site in Poltava, with electricity interruptions, damage to a DTEK thermal facility and reported disruption to the external power supply to Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant. No radiation release is established. Separate reports describe casualties and civilian, retail and logistics impacts in Zaporizhzhia, Odesa and the Kyiv region; named businesses should verify direct site impacts before changing forecasts. The Council of the EU adopted new dual-use export restrictions covering 15 Russia-linked entities on November 25. This enacted measure is distinct from the Commission's still-proposed 22nd Russia sanctions package. European Commission leaders also urged a winter energy ceasefire and safer Black Sea food shipping, but no implemented ceasefire was established. Newly documented Polish security responses from November 23 and 24 are retained with their original event dates. Yesterday's UK sabotage alert and Finnish reporting on Russia's Kamenka military construction remain dated background, not proof of an imminent NATO attack.
