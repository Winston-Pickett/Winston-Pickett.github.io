Inside this edition

New policy proposals, operational disruptions and verified decision gates.

The November 19 edition separates the European Commission's €45 billion Ukraine financing proposal from approved legislation and actual cash disbursement. Russian officials report a third Crimean Bridge attack this week and suspended railway service, with independently verified November 17 photographs distinguished from current restoration notices. Tatarstan district officials report a drone attack on an oil facility without a major fire; Reuters' separate refinery-throughput estimates concern cumulative earlier attacks, not new November 19 events. A Coalition of the Willing session in Berlin is scheduled for November 19, but the report cutoff precedes any verified outcome. The country-by-country business outlook translates actual transport, energy, sanctions, insurance and treasury exposures into evidence-based management triggers. The complete 91-page report preserves ten numbered sections, a 357-record rolling register, 22 separate date-limited disclosures, nine standing indicators, and source-linked citations.
