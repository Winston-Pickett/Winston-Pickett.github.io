Inside this edition

Verified events, qualified attribution and practical business readiness.

The revised November 15 edition examines separately dated Black Sea merchant-vessel incidents near Romania, Ukraine's electricity and civilian-infrastructure disruption, attributed industrial damage in Russia and the latest competing negotiation positions. The risk outlook distinguishes vessel-specific maritime effects from a regional shipping closure and separates official reports of electricity disruption from company-specific losses. It retains 341 dated/verified-overlap records and 22 separately date-limited disclosures, along with the complete standing-indicator dashboard. The revised edition supersedes the earlier November 15 Word draft.
