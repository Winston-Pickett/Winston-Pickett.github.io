Ukraine electricity and Black Sea freight disruption require site-level continuity checks.

The November 26 edition examines new reported electricity interruptions affecting seven Ukrainian regions, damage to two grain vessels near Odesa, proposed Polish aviation restrictions, joint European armoured-vehicle procurement and a NATO-US diplomatic statement emphasizing avoidance of wider war. It also separates an earlier Swiss cyber advisory and a conditional Lukoil asset transaction from implemented measures. No newly verified material change in combat-ready Russian formations near NATO was established in the reviewed sources.
