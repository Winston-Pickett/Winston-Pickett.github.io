Method and fairness safeguards

Claims are selected for materiality, testability and public significance, without artificial partisan quotas. Each documented speaker/date/event/proposition receives a distinct immutable occurrence ID, and equal normalized propositions share a stable cross-reference. Co-signed letters are not presented as independent oral speeches.

Evidence findings distinguish supported by documented evidence, contradicted by documented evidence, partly supported/context missing, and not presently verifiable. Predictions and opinions are distinguished from established events, and the record does not infer a speaker's intent to mislead without direct proof. Previous reports remain historical audit snapshots.
