01 / Executive bottom line

Disruptions are documented. Coordinated warfare is not established.

The most consequential recent developments relate to infrastructure resilience, recovery and the attribution of known incidents. Federal investigators reported an explosive device at the BioUrja ethanol facility in Peoria, Illinois, but no perpetrator or motive had been established by the publication cutoff. The FAA's nationwide ground stop had a preliminary technical explanation involving telecommunications infrastructure. A tanker collision in New Orleans remained under investigation, and a regional electricity outage was added to the register.

For leaders, the immediate issue is not speculative escalation. It is whether communications, payments, transport, industrial safety and essential-services dependencies can withstand disruption and recover predictably. Financial planning should test the consequences of delayed shipments, emergency transportation, overtime, cash settlement exceptions and concentrated vendors. No reviewed case in this issue establishes a coordinated foreign-directed campaign against U.S. infrastructure.

Interpretation: Incident confirmation, malicious intent, attribution and operational severity are separate questions. Silence or uncertainty from investigators is not evidence of a foreign actor.

06 / Editorial policy

Methods, attribution and corrections

Scope. The editorial search considers states, Washington, D.C., and territories, but its source review is uneven. Public operator statements, government investigations and independent reporting were consulted. Unreported or classified intrusions, local incidents and inaccessible primary reports are significant blind spots.

Evidence standard. A confirmed incident is not a confirmed motive. H1 records show substantiated malicious activity; H2 records show documented events with unresolved hostile character. D1 records have a supported non-hostile cause; D2 records have an unresolved initiating cause. These codes are independent of operational severity and any attribution to foreign actors.

Changes since prior edition. New US26-0031; Peoria US26-0029 revised H2 to H1 following reporting of FBI confirmation; technical and recovery notes refined for FAA US26-0028 and New Orleans US26-0030. Zero IDs deleted. The register is persistent and selective, not exhaustive.

Separately watched items. US26-0014 is a contextual alleged thwarted plot rather than an executed disruption. US26-PLN-0001 covers announced Amtrak maintenance and is not counted as an emergency event.
