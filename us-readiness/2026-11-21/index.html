Executive assessment

Verify actual disruption, isolate cause, and prepare proportionately.

The strongest ongoing localized interruption is Spartanburg Water's drinking-water restriction, still under a boil-water advisory at the utility's November 20 evening update. Four previously unrecorded Bandwidth telecom and 911-related events from November 17-20 are backfilled with stable IDs US26-0054 through US26-0057. Bandwidth reports all four incidents resolved. RedSky's West Virginia 911 routing notice is resolved, but the Cincinnati-specific notice lacks a posted closure in the checked operator report. None of those notices alone establishes sabotage or state involvement.

DOE's November 20 emergency order retains Eddystone generating capacity in Pennsylvania for regional resource adequacy starting November 21. That is an energy reliability directive, not evidence of a current blackout.
