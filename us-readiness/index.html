Why this publication exists

Prepare with perspective.

Domestic Readiness Daily helps executives evaluate disruptions without automatically treating incidents as warfare. The focus is evidence, operating dependencies, root causes, measurable consequences and proportional preparation.

01 / People & assets

Protect what matters.

Understand credible site-specific security concerns and essential-service outages without overstating attribution or intent.

02 / Operations

Maintain continuity.

Monitor transport, cyber, energy, telecom, healthcare and manufacturing disruptions, including non-hostile failures.

03 / Finance & leadership

Plan with discipline.

Translate verified business impacts into cash, forecasts, staffing, procurement, insurance and recovery decisions.

Coverage: The United States, including the states, Washington, D.C., and relevant territories, with unequal visibility across sources. The 60-day incident register is a selected documented sample, not an exhaustive national census.

Reading the evidence

Disruption is not proof of warfare.

Each incident is assessed separately for occurrence, malicious action, root cause, operational severity and attribution. The editorial codes below are not national threat scores or determinations of foreign sponsorship.

H1

Confirmed malicious activity, but not automatically terrorism or warfare.

H2

Suspected hostile involvement; the malicious character remains unestablished.

D1

Major disruption with a supported accidental, technical or environmental cause.

D2

Documented disruption whose initiating cause remains unresolved.

Independent public-source planning. Not an official intelligence warning, a declaration of terrorism, or a forecast of warfare. Historic findings retain their original evidence cutoff.
