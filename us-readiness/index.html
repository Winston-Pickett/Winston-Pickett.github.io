Editorial mission

Security reporting with operational discipline.

Verify

Report the observed incident, its date and its consequences. Separate confirmed occurrences from allegations, investigations and incomplete disclosures.

Explain

Track technical, environmental, accidental and malicious root causes without assuming that disruption is evidence of coordinated warfare.

Prepare

Translate documented dependencies into finance, operations, cyber, procurement, workforce and executive decisions, with escalation triggers.

Reading the evidence

The incident classification legend

The code describes whether hostile action is established, suspected or absent from the known cause. It is not a severity rating or a determination of foreign-state responsibility.

H1

Malicious activity confirmed

A deliberate harmful act is substantiated. It does not necessarily establish terrorism or warfare.

H2

Hostile activity suspected

The disruption is documented; whether malicious action occurred remains under investigation.

D1

Non-hostile disruption

A substantial interruption has a supported accidental, environmental, technical or operational cause.

D2

Cause unresolved

The incident is verified, but the initiating root cause is unknown or insufficiently substantiated.

Example: A D1 nationwide technology outage may cause more business disruption than an H1 incident at one facility. Operational severity and attribution are assessed separately.

Accountability

Scope, corrections and limitations

Coverage is eligible nationwide, including states, Washington, D.C. and U.S. territories, but source access is uneven. The 60-day register is a selective historical backfill rather than a complete inventory of U.S. disruptions. One carry-in began before the window and remains because effects continued into it.

H1 indicates malicious action established, not necessarily terrorism or warfare. Attribution to a state or foreign actor is an independent assessment and is not inferred from the technical method used. We retain permanent incident IDs, record corrections and identify gaps where a primary source was unavailable.
