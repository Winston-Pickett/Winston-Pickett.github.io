Executive assessment

Separate verified events from speculation. Protect the exposed dependency.

The new November 20 master documents third-party data-security disclosures affecting JPMorgan Chase and Mastercard programs, an Alaska snow-related outage, a newly backfilled Sacramento electricity disruption, and a further FAA account of an earlier nationwide aviation-system failure.
