## Required source sweep
Before marking a region/sector clear, review actual incident notices, **including recently resolved and historical pages**, for the prior 72 hours; search regional/local news for the prior 7 days to identify retrospective omissions. Six geographic groups: Northeast; Southeast; Midwest; South/Central; West (incl. AK/HI); national/multiregion, plus explicit US territory check. Nine monitored sectors: energy/pipelines; water/wastewater; telecom/911; transportation/logistics; cyber/cloud; healthcare; banking/payments; industrial/manufacturing; public services. This is a coverage plan, not a claim that all sources were accessed.
Priority direct sources: PHMSA and National Response Center for pipeline/release signals; local utilities and state drinking-water agencies for water; RedSky/Bandwidth/carrier status histories and local PSAPs for 911; Brightline, BNSF/CSX, FAA, NTSB and USCG for transport; CISA, FBI, HHS, vendor/status histories and SEC 8-Ks for cyber/data; DOE/NERC, operators, state emergency authorities and high-quality regional outlets for material outages. Record actual URLs and retrieval times. Initial emergency reports are preliminary and must be corroborated where possible; no proof of a malicious attack is inferred from an unexplained outage.

## Daily morning release gate
1. Open all four approved European and U.S. WORKFLOW / FORMAT_STANDARD files (reference only; do not edit Europe). Load latest fully published U.S. Word/PDF, `editions.json`, and candidate queue. If a source is missing, disclose the exact gap; **do not** substitute a blank register.
2. Sweep sector sources and regional coverage, record every qualifying candidate and any area unassessed or inaccessible. Run searches both without known operator names and against named operators to reduce hindsight bias. Ensure today's overnight period and a 7-day lookback are covered.
3. Triage by documented business continuity significance; a fire, water outage, pipeline isolation, public-safety telecom interruption, transport closure, major cloud outage or hospital diversion may qualify without proven malicious cause. Class H1/H2/D1/D2 and foreign attribution independently. Avoid padding with routine outages and vulnerabilities.
4. Reconcile every queue item: `published`, `excluded` with evidence/reason, `watch_only`, or explicitly `qualified_unpublished` / `needs_recheck` with reason and next check. A **known, verified material qualified_unpublished** item blocks claiming complete daily coverage; include it or state a publication hold/failure. Compare old IDs and issue dates to prevent duplicates and recycled news.
5. Create **one Word master** from approved Word visual system with full 60-day register, ten sections, 9 stacked indicator cards, region-by-sector exposures, changed-since-prior, primary risk type prefixes, citations and honest coverage disclosure. Export PDF from Word; inspect every page, links, text selection, counts and source cutoffs. A quiet day still gets a correctly scoped report.
6. GitHub: upload final PDF, write dated page, update `editions.json` and homepage Latest/Archive; preserve archived PDFs; verify binary and metadata readback. Check **GitHub Pages deployment job success** and fetch the public dated page and homepage (not merely the Git commit). If deployment fails, report a publishing failure, not success; retry only with evidence-backed action. Do not alter site-wide protected design to evade a check. Record whether a separate site-standard workflow failed.
7. Do not email or post to LinkedIn without authorization applicable to this run. Do not change Europe Readiness.
8. Publish measured indicators: sources checked/unassessed/inaccessible, candidates added/qualified/published/excluded/watch, detection delay where both real timestamps exist, 60-day additions/changes/age-offs, and live deploy status. No made-up complete national scan claim.

## Afternoon exception control
Review operator incident histories, regional infrastructure news and latest agency notices since the morning cutoff, plus a rolling 7-day retrospective gap search. Update candidate queue and confirm prior closures. For a materially significant verified new event or status correction, alert with a factual source link, event time, category, uncertainty and publication recommendation. Do not silently rewrite the morning edition or publish external files without separately authorized release; keep minor issues for next morning. No alert if the sweep genuinely finds no material change, but record reviewed coverage.
